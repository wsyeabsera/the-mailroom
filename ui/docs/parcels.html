Parcels and approvals

A parcel is one request. This page shows every state it can be in, what moves it from one to the next, and how to approve or decline one safely.

A parcel starts as queued, waiting_approval or refused, depending on the contract. Queued parcels go to the laptop and end as done or failed. A parcel that waits for approval is "Needs you": you read what it will do, then approve or decline. Refused and cancelled parcels are final.

The life of a parcel

The states in plain words

Colours match the admin app: green for done, amber for waiting approval, red for refused and failed, grey for the rest.

The code lists an expired state in some places, but the Hub never sets it. A parcel whose contract has run out is refused with the reason "contract expired". Cancelled parcels have no tab on the Parcels page, but they show under "All".

A client may label a parcel (for example test). The label shows as a small tag next to the task, so test runs are easy to tell from real work.

"Needs you"

"Needs you" means a parcel is in waiting_approval: the contract allows it, but marks that task as one you must approve. It shows up in three places:

  • The Needs you card on the Overview page.
  • The Approvals page, which says "holding at the counter until you say so".
  • The toolbar badge of the browser extension.

Approving safely

An approval is the one moment where you stand between a risky action and your files. Take ten seconds.

  1. Open Approvals. Each waiting parcel shows who asked (the key's label), the task, and what it wants to touch.
  2. Press Review. The page "What it will do" lists the task, the path or folder, and the effect. For write_file it shows the size, such as "Writes 120 bytes", and the full content. For claude it shows the prompt and the folder.
  3. Check three things: is this the key you expect, is the path where you expect, and is the content what you would write yourself?
  4. Press Approve to send it on, or Decline to refuse it.

A claude task may also carry optional settings: allowedTools (up to 20 tool names), maxTurns (1 to 50), model, and json (return Claude's JSON output instead of plain text). Invalid values make the job fail.

Approving a claude task lets Claude Code act in that folder with whatever permissions claude -p has on your laptop. The contract limits the folder, not what Claude does inside it. Read the prompt.

  • Approve moves the parcel to queued. The Hub checks the contract once more first, so a parcel that no longer fits becomes refused instead.
  • Decline makes it refused with the reason "declined by owner". The app is told.
  • If someone else (or you, in another tab) already decided, the app says "This parcel is no longer waiting". You cannot decide twice.

Make a contract with write_file ticked under both "Allowed tasks" and "Needs your approval". Send a write_file parcel with curl. Open Approvals, press Review, and read the bytes and content. Approve it, then open the file on your laptop.

When a parcel is refused

Open the parcel on the Parcels page. The panel Why it was refused (or "Why it was declined") shows the reason text. When the parcel has a path or a folder, it also shows two lines: what the parcel asked for, and the folders the contract allows. It does this for any refusal, not only a path one, except when the reason is exactly "declined by owner".

  1. Read the reason. Common ones are contract expired, task “...” is not in the contract, daily limit of N reached, and ... is outside the contract.
  2. If the app was wrong, fix the app and leave the contract alone.
  3. If the contract was too tight, press Widen the contract. It opens that contract's editor. Add the folder or task and save.
  4. Have the app send the parcel again.

A refused parcel stays refused, even after you widen the contract. Nothing re-runs it. The app has to send a new parcel. Widen only as far as you mean to: a wider contract applies to every parcel from every key on it.

The two lines and the button are not shown when the reason is exactly "declined by owner", which is what you get by pressing Decline in the admin app. A decline made through Claude or another MCP app with a note is stored as "declined by owner: <note>", and that one does show them and the button. A parcel refused by the laptop's check shows the laptop's reason, for example "resolves outside the contract folders".

Under the hood: what each change writes
  • Every state change adds one event: job.queued, job.waiting_approval, job.refused, job.approved, job.declined, job.running, job.done, job.failed, job.cancelled. The "Tracking" list on a parcel is made from them.
  • When the laptop disconnects, running parcels return to queued with the reason "laptop disconnected".
  • Only one parcel runs at a time. The Hub sends the next after the previous result.
  • The app reads its parcel with GET /api/jobs/<id>, and sees only parcels from its own key.
