The admin app

The admin app is the website where you, the owner, run the Mailroom: see what arrived, approve risky steps, and hand out or take back keys.

It has eight pages in three groups, plus a link to these docs. Work is what is happening now. Access is who may send. System is the laptop and the log. Only you can open it, with one password. The screenshots below are from a throwaway test copy with made-up data.

How the app is laid out

Every page has its own address after a #, such as /#/approvals. That means the browser's back button and bookmarks work.

Signing in

Open the Hub's address. You see "Owner entrance" and one password box. There is no user name: there is only one owner.

  • A wrong password tells you how many tries are left.
  • After 5 wrong passwords from the same address within 15 minutes, the door locks. The page shows "Door is locked" with a countdown. The 15 minutes count from the first wrong try.
  • Sign out in the top bar ends the session and returns you to this page.

The pages

Overview

The home page. It greets you, says in one line how things are, and shows what needs you.

  • Needs you: parcels waiting for your yes, each with Approve and Review.
  • Waiting for the laptop: shown only when the laptop is away.
  • Today: counts of done, running, queued, refused and failed. The finished ones count from midnight UTC.

Parcels

The newest parcels (up to 200), newest first. Filter tabs (All, Waiting for you, Queued, Running, Done, Refused, Failed) show how many of those are in each state, and a tab with a zero count is hidden. Click a row to open one parcel: a step-by-step Tracking timeline, the contract check, what was asked and the result.

On a refused parcel, Widen the contract opens the contract editor. Only do that if you really want the key to do it.

Approvals

Parcels that wait for you. Each card shows the key, the task, the path and a one-line preview of the change. Approve sends it on, Decline refuses it (reason "declined by owner"), Review opens the full text of what it will do and the contract that asked for approval.

Keys

One key per client app. The list shows label, contract, last used, created and status (active or revoked). New key asks for a label and a contract, then shows the key once.

A key's page lists its contract and recent parcels. Revoke key stops it at once and cancels its queued, waiting and running parcels. It cannot be undone: you hand out a new key.

Contracts

A contract says what a key may do: folders, tasks, a daily limit, an expiry and which tasks need approval. The editor shows In plain words on the right, a sentence that updates as you type. Saving applies to the next parcel. Queued parcels are checked again just before the Hub sends them to the laptop, and waiting ones when you approve. Running parcels are not stopped, and a refused parcel is never re-run.

Mistakes show next to the field and as a banner ("Not saved. Fix 2 things below."). Only tasks you allow can be set to need approval.

Tokens

Tokens let other apps and Claude sessions manage the Mailroom from outside. They are separate from client keys. A token is read only unless you also tick write or approve. The page warns that whoever holds approve can release a risky step without you reading it. A new token is shown once, with ready-to-paste commands. See Claude and other apps.

Laptop

Shows whether the daemon is connected, when it was last seen, the credential hint (only its last four characters) and the queue. Rotate credential asks you to confirm, then shows the new one once. See The laptop and the daemon.

Events

The log of everything that happened, read-only. It loads the latest 100 events and then streams new ones live, with the newest at the top. It updates live: the badge next to the title says Live. If the line drops it says "Reconnecting...", shows "Lost the line" and offers Catch up, which fetches what you missed. See Events.

Docs

The rail's Help group links to these docs. They are behind the same sign-in.

Empty, loading and error states

  • Loading: grey placeholder bars where the content will be.
  • Empty: a short message and one button, for example "No keys yet. Nobody can knock until you hand out a key." with Hand out a key.
  • Error: "The post got lost on the way", the reason, and Try again. If a whole page fails to load you see "This page failed to load.".
  • Decisions: a green banner such as "Decision recorded." after an action. If a parcel is no longer waiting (someone else decided first), you get "is no longer waiting" and nothing changes.

Dark, Light or System

The switch in the top bar (and on the sign-in page and in these docs) has three settings. Dark and Light are fixed. System follows your computer. The choice is remembered in this browser only, so a different browser starts on System.

Open the Approvals page with nothing waiting. You see "The counter is clear.". Then open Parcels and click the Refused tab to see what the Hub has turned away.
