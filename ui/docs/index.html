The Mailroom

A small private service that lets your other software ask the Claude Code on your laptop to do work, safely. Every request is checked twice: once in the cloud, and again on your laptop.

Think of an office mailroom. Software drops a parcel at the front desk. The desk checks it against the sender's contract. Safe parcels go to your laptop, risky ones wait for you to say yes, and anything outside the rules is turned away and written down.
